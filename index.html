BREAK N EGG

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Break N Egg is an offline game. The game does not collect or send your personal information to us. Your game progress and settings are stored on your device.

This policy applies to the Android game Break N Egg, package com.breakNegg.game, provided by NativeSpark Studio.

Information the game handles

You do not need an account to play. The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, microphone recordings or advertising identifier. It does not include advertising, analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

The game stores progress and preferences locally, including game state, unlocked skills, rewards, tutorial progress and sound and performance settings. This information is used to resume your game and remember your choices; the game does not transmit it to us.

Device permissions

The game uses the device’s vibration feature for haptic feedback. This does not collect information about you. The current Android release does not request internet access.

Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell or share personal information collected through gameplay, because the game does not collect it. The game does not operate an online account or cloud-save service.

Google Play and Android may process information for app distribution, account management, security and platform diagnostics under their own policies and your device settings. See Google’s Privacy Policy for information about Google’s services.

Storage and deletion

Game progress and settings remain in the app’s local storage until they are reset or removed. You can remove the game’s local data by clearing its storage in Android settings or uninstalling the game. This also removes your saved progress from that installation.

Because we do not receive your local game data, we cannot access, restore or delete it remotely.

Children’s privacy

The game’s no-collection approach applies to players of all ages. It does not ask children to provide personal information. If you believe personal information about a child has been sent to us in a support message, contact us so we can address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or our privacy practices change. We will publish the updated policy on this page and revise its effective date.

Support messages and privacy requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Our email service provider processes these messages to deliver and store them. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

We limit access to support correspondence to the people handling your request. We keep it only as long as needed to provide support and meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your correspondence using the email address below.
